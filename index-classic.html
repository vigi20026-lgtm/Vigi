Project Introduction

VIGI LOCK is a proposed Android application that combines cybersecurity awareness and student productivity. Target users: college students and everyday smartphone users who want understandable security guidance and better study organisation.

Module 1 · Cybersecurity Assistant
  • URL input validation and suspicious-link review
  • Supported-file inspection
  • Risk explanations and safety recommendations
Module 2 · Student Study Assistant
  • Daily timetables, subjects, assignments, revision
  • Study reminders and progress tracking
  • Voice assistance

Problem Statement

Cybersecurity problems
  • Phishing messages and suspicious links
  • Fake login pages
  • Unexpected APK files
  • Confusing security warnings
Student problems
  • Missed study sessions and forgotten assignments
  • Unorganised timetables, irregular revision
  • Difficulty tracking progress

Fictional examples only. Benefits are goals, not guarantees.
